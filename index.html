Built to be safe

Nobody gets your location. Ever.

The ring works without a single person ever seeing where you are.

Your location stays yours

Your exact location is never shared with another user. No map, no pin, no distance readout. We only check whether a match is within a few meters.

Ghost mode, instantly

One switch pauses the ring and hides you from discovery. Location and Bluetooth each have their own toggle too. Turn it on whenever you want to disappear.

No trackers, no selling

No third-party advertising or analytics trackers, and we never sell your personal information. Delete your account any time. Deletion is permanent and immediate.

Students only

Everyone here actually goes to your school

Verify once with your school sign-in or your .edu email, and you're in. You only ever see and match with students from your own campus.

V

Verified students carry the badge. It confirms they're enrolled where they say they are. It isn't a background check, so always use good judgment when you meet someone.
